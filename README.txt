HOW TO DEPLOY:
1. Unzip this package.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Upload EVERYTHING inside to the root of the GitHub repository connected to Vercel:
   index.html
   styles.css
   script.js
   news.js
   favicon.svg
   vercel.json
   README.txt
   assets/
4. Commit to main.
5. Vercel should deploy automatically.
6. Open the newest Ready deployment and click Visit.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT upload the ZIP itself as the website.
- Do NOT leave out assets/.
- Do NOT leave out news.js, styles.css, or script.js.
