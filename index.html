ABOUT PUP KEYOTI

The wild coyote behind the sound.

Pup Keyoti is an independent artist and creator blending bass-heavy electronic energy, pop, trap and modern country influence into a sound that refuses to sit in one box.

Underneath the chaos is a simple idea: turn survival into momentum, turn outsiders into community, and make something people can feel before they even know the words.

The result is The Den — part music universe, part creator ecosystem, part gaming community, and completely its own thing.
